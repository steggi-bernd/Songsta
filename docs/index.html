The feel of a streaming app, for the music you own.

Songsta is a music player for Windows, Linux and Android. It brings your files, Jellyfin and Navidrome together in one library, with Hi-Res FLAC, synced lyrics, mixes made from your own listening and the speakers in your home.

Everything a streaming app does, built from your own music

A Home page with mixes, artist pages, a real queue and one stream that moves between your devices, for the songs on your disk and your server.

One library from everywhere

Local folders, Jellyfin, Navidrome and any Subsonic server side by side. A song you have twice is listed once and plays from your disk first.

Made for good files

FLAC up to 24-bit/192 kHz, and the player bar shows what really plays. Leveling, crossfade and an equalizer with AutoEQ for 9,000 headphones.

Mixes from your listening

On Repeat, Rediscover, Calm, Upbeat and a mix per genre, smart playlists that fill themselves, and mixes that flow from song to song.

Synced lyrics

From your files, your server or LRCLIB, searchable by a line you remember, and a full-screen view with a visualizer that moves with the music.

A queue that listens

First come, first played, shuffle that goes one way, and a song that branches off into the rest of its list.

On your phone

The Android app plays your server and your folders, with downloads for when there's no connection.

One stream everywhere

Playlists and where you stopped stay in sync, and one device steers another, like Spotify Connect.

Coming from Spotify?

Your Liked Songs and playlists come over in their order, with a list of what you still want to buy.

Sounds like: find what fits

Pick a song and Songsta finds the songs in your library that sound like it, by tempo, key and energy measured from the music itself. Turn the Camelot wheel to hear another key, and play the matches as a mix that flows from one song into the next.

  • Same tempo, same key, both, or same energy
  • The Camelot wheel DJs use, with key correction
  • Mixes by sound: late night, run, focus, sunlit

Replay: your year, told back

Your month, your year or all of it as a story: the albums you played most, five songs you couldn't stop, your prime time, the pulse of the period and the faces of your top artists.

With the optional sync service, Replay counts what all your devices played.

Every speaker in the house

Receivers and network speakers over UPnP/DLNA, Chromecast, Google TV and Nest: one press moves the music, and it goes on from the same place in the song.

  • Several speakers at once, each with its own volume
  • A different song in each room
  • Receivers and TVs that switch themselves off afterwards

On your phone too

The Android app looks and works like the desktop app: your server's music and the folders on your phone, downloads for when there's no connection, the lock screen and headset buttons, and the speakers at home.

It offers its own updates, or follow them with Obtainium.

Install in a minute

The desktop app stands on its own. The phone app and the sync service build on your Jellyfin server.

Windows

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit

  1. Download and run Songsta-Setup.exe.
  2. The installer isn't code-signed, so SmartScreen asks once: More info → Run anyway.
  3. Songsta updates itself from then on.

Yours, and nobody else's

Songsta has no servers of its own. Your library and your listening stay on your devices, and on your own server if you run one.

No account, no ads, no analytics

Nothing to sign up for, nothing that watches what you play.

You decide what goes online

Settings list every outside service Songsta may use, what it sends and why. Allow all, only your own servers, or pick.

No passwords kept

Your server sign-ins are kept as tokens, sealed by Windows or your Linux desktop's keyring.

Questions

Is Songsta free?

Yes. Songsta is open source under the GNU GPL v3, with no account, no ads and nothing to buy.

Do I need a server?

No. The desktop app plays the folders on your computer on its own, and Jellyfin, Navidrome or another Subsonic server can join the same library. The phone app plays your Jellyfin server, folders on the phone, or both.

Is Songsta a Spotify alternative?

For the music you own. It doesn't stream a catalogue; it plays your files and servers with the comfort of a streaming app. Your Spotify Liked Songs and playlists can come over, with a list of what you still want to buy.

Which formats does Songsta play?

MP3, AAC, FLAC up to 24-bit/192 kHz, WAV, Ogg Vorbis and Opus directly; AIFF, ALAC, WMA, WavPack, APE and DSD with FFmpeg.

Is there a version for macOS or iPhone?

Not yet. Songsta runs on Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit Linux and Android 7 or newer.

Why does Windows warn me before installing?

The installer isn't code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen asks once. Choose More info, then Run anyway. The source code and every release are public on GitHub.

Does Songsta collect data?

No. There is no analytics. What goes online (open music databases, lyrics, the update check) is listed in Settings → Network & privacy, where you can switch each service off.
